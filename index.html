About Lone Thug

Lone Thug is a men's clothing brand built on one idea: put a name on your clothes that people can read from across the room and feel when they touch it.

We don't follow the crowd. We dress the ones who move on their own.

Lone Thug started with a single tee and a logo patch sewn on by hand. People stopped us to ask where it came from. That patch became the brand.

Today we make five lines: T-Shirts, Polos, Caps, Office and Up & Down. Every piece is designed in-house and produced in small batches, so what you wear stays rare.

The mark

Stitched, not printed

Our logo patch is cut from felt, outlined in red and sewn on. It will not crack, peel or fade in the wash.

The fit

FAQ

Answers about orders, delivery, payment, sizing and returns. Still need help? Message us on WhatsApp.

Orders

How do I place an order?

Open the piece you want, choose the colour, size and quantity, then tap Order on WhatsApp. Your order message is written for you. Send it and our team will confirm stock, the total with delivery, and your delivery date.

Can I order more than one item?

Yes. Send each piece in the same WhatsApp chat, or list them in one message. We combine them into one order and one delivery fee.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can change or cancel free of charge until your order is dispatched. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number as soon as possible.

Do you take custom or bulk orders?

Yes. We produce team, event and corporate orders from 10 pieces, with your choice of colours. Lead time is usually 10 to 14 working days. Message us with the piece, colours, sizes and quantity for a quote.

Will a sold-out item come back?

Most core pieces are restocked. Limited pieces may not return. Join the email list or follow us on Instagram to hear first.

Shipping

Where do you deliver?

We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT. International delivery is available on request; message us for a quote.

How long does delivery take?

Major cities: 2 to 4 working days. Other locations: 3 to 7 working days. Orders confirmed before 12 noon on a working day are dispatched the same day.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is calculated by location and confirmed before you pay. Delivery is free on orders over ₦75,000.

How do I track my order?

Once your order is dispatched we send your tracking details on WhatsApp.

Payment

How can I pay?

Bank transfer to our business account, or card payment through a secure payment link. Pay on delivery is available in selected cities.

Is it safe to pay?

We only ask for payment to our registered business account or through a secure link we send in the same WhatsApp chat. We will never ask for your card PIN or OTP.

Sizing and fit

How do your pieces fit?

Our tees, polos and sets are cut boxy and relaxed. Take your usual size for the intended look, or one size down for a closer fit. See the size guide.

I'm between sizes. What should I pick?

Size up for a baggier look, size down for a cleaner fit. Send your height and weight on WhatsApp and we'll recommend a size.

Are the caps adjustable?

Yes. All caps are one size with an adjustable strap at the back.

Returns and care

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. You can exchange within 7 days of delivery if the piece is unworn, unwashed and has its tags. Read the full returns and exchanges policy.

Will the patch come off in the wash?

No. The patch is stitched on. Wash cold and inside out, and never iron directly on the patch. See the care guide.

What if my item arrives damaged or wrong?

Message us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We will replace the item or refund you in full, including delivery.

Returns & exchanges

Wrong size or changed your mind? Here is how it works.

Exchanges

You can exchange any item for a different size or colour within 7 days of delivery, subject to stock.

Conditions

  • The item is unworn, unwashed and free of perfume, makeup or marks.
  • All original tags are attached and the item is in its original packaging.
  • Custom, personalised and sale items cannot be returned or exchanged unless faulty.

How to start an exchange

  1. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and the item you want instead.
  2. We confirm the exchange and send the return details.
  3. Send the item back. Once we receive and check it, we dispatch the replacement within 2 working days.

Return delivery costs

For size or colour exchanges, the customer covers the return delivery. We cover the cost of sending the replacement once.

Refunds

We offer refunds for items that arrive damaged, faulty or incorrect. Report the issue with photos within 48 hours of delivery. Approved refunds are paid to your bank account within 5 working days.

How to measure

  • Chest: lay a tee you love flat and measure straight across, armpit to armpit. Double it.
  • Length: from the highest point of the shoulder to the hem.
  • Waist: measure straight across the waistband and double it.

Care guide

Keep your pieces and the patch looking new.

FAQShipping policyReturns and exchangesPaymentSize guideCare guidePrivacy policyTerms of service

Washing

  • Turn the item inside out before washing.
  • Machine wash cold (30°C) or hand wash with similar colours.
  • Use a mild detergent. Do not bleach.

Drying

  • Air dry in the shade. Direct sun fades dark colours over time.
  • Do not tumble dry. It can shrink cotton and loosen the patch edges.

Ironing

Iron inside out on medium heat. Never iron directly on the patch.

Caps

Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not machine wash. Stuff with paper to keep the shape while drying.

Office wear

Dry clean the Relaxed Suit and Senator Set for best results, or hand wash cold and hang to dry.
